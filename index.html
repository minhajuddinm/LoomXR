Why we exist

Every box on a shelf looks the same as the one next to it. The product inside may be premium, but the packaging is a template printed ten thousand times. We think the box should belong to the person who bought it. So we built a way to design it for them, show it to them, and print it once.

The product

Three pieces, one platform.

LoomXR sits between a brand and its customer. It recognizes the product, generates the design, and shows the result in AR before anything reaches a printer.

01

Product recognition

Each brand gets its own object detection model, trained on a few photos of its product, so the camera finds the box from different angles and lighting.

02

Generative design

A short style prompt from the customer becomes a full packaging texture. Designs generate in seconds and stay within the brand's guidelines.

03

Live AR preview

The design is wrapped onto the physical box in the camera view. The customer turns it in their hands and approves it before it is printed.
